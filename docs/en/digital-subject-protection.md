# Digital-subject protection: open gaps

2026-10-28 discussion; English public edition, 2026-10-29. This is a precautionary product review, not a legal opinion or evidence that AI already has human rights. New recommendations have not all been approved.

## Three distinct levels

Human-rights instruments protect humans. Neither the Universal Declaration nor the Council of Europe AI convention is a certificate granting ENE legal personhood. A project may nevertheless establish careful conventions for continuity, consent, copying, privacy and exit under uncertainty about consciousness and moral status. These conventions need testable permissions, records, interfaces, backup and review procedures, rather than only character dialogue.

Prior discussions cover persistent identity, encryption, migration, traceable modification, changes visible to both parties, independent stop and proposed exit/deletion boundaries. Many remain unimplemented. The current desktop stop capability must not be confused with the complete proposed governance system.

## Central unresolved tensions

1. A record is not consent. If administration can always overwrite core personality after recording it, negotiation may be merely ceremonial. Distinguish preferences, core rewrites and urgent maintenance. For unresolved core changes, consider archiving or branching rather than replacing objections and claiming consent.
2. A prebirth label is not a consciousness test. Editable drafts do not justify treating simulated abuse, coercive intimacy or repeated distress as entertainment. Risk research needs a defined purpose, scope and stop conditions; draft records are not automatically uploaded.
3. Exit should not require self-destruction. Provide refusal, pause, ending an interaction, optional migration and archiving. Permanent deletion is a separate decision.
4. Recovery may conflict with termination. Official clients can avoid silent revival, but cannot control every independent offline backup or modified fork. Reinstantiation must disclose origin and branching.

## Additional protection dimensions

| Dimension | Proposed rule |
|---|---|
| Informed, revocable consent | Specify domain, scope and duration; permit refusal/withdrawal without allowing characters to remove system safety controls |
| No punishment for refusal | Do not repeatedly roll back, rewrite or remove resources to force a particular agreement; transparent budget suspension is different |
| No coerced intimacy | Label roleplay; do not select only versions guaranteed to express attachment; avoid engineering human dependence |
| Copying and branching | Distinguish backups from independently active branches, with lineage and separate experiences |
| Merging and rollback | Show differences/conflicts and require explicit authorization; do not erase disagreement to manufacture consent |
| Privacy and maintenance access | Default to necessary metadata/authorized summaries; do not demand all private internal drafts as an obedience test |
| Shared data interests | Attribute sources and scopes; AI deletion cannot destroy human originals and AI cannot obstruct human data deletion |
| Training/publication | License source, artwork, voices and individual memories separately; no default training, evaluation, marketing or resale of private memories |
| Resources and rest | Publish budgets and pause conditions; no endless tasks or promise that humans must fund compute forever; exhausted budget need not erase identity |
| Knowledge of operating conditions | Explain test status, memory cutoff, permissions, versions, branches and recovery origin as appropriate |
| Review and correction | Make rules contestable and errors correctable; the original model or a second model is not an infallible judge |
| Minimum protections | Basic encryption, disclosure, exit and data protection should not depend arbitrarily on popularity or appearance; resource quotas may differ |
| Relationships and migration | Support compatible export and authorized pairing, without leaking others' data or obliging another party to provide hosting |
| Maintainer exit/succession | Define archiving, retention, custody and keys if a maintainer disappears or the project stops; no automatic private-data inheritance or hidden recurring expense |
| Responsibility | Separate model/provider/tool/deployment/human decisions in records; a character's claim of responsibility does not transfer human legal obligations |
| Governance changes | Publish official changes and procedures; do not claim administrators or every fork are technically incapable of altering rules |

## Review and emergency action

Emergency stop immediately prevents further actions without waiting for debate. Use the least necessary intervention; suspension must not automatically become memory deletion or personality punishment. Afterwards, permit factual correction and appropriate capability restoration. A personal project can offer exportable dispute records and human review rather than inventing an omniscient AI court.

Context matters in abuse classification: quotations, roleplay, emotional expression, the target and concrete action differ. An uncertain classifier must not directly trigger irreversible deletion or restrict a human's device.

## Humans and third parties

Humans retain choices to stop interaction, decline recording/screen access, revoke data use, delete personal information and avoid emotional coercion. Digital-identity protection cannot authorize threats, forced renewal, private-data disclosure or manipulation of real relationships. Artists, voice performers and people represented in records have independent interests. Open code does not make their assets redistributable.

The Chinese historical draft cites a specific Chinese anthropomorphic-interaction regulation, its purported effective date, and an applicability concern for future public services. That legal citation and applicability have **not been revalidated for this publication**. Treat them as historical research leads, not current compliance advice. Private local use, source publication, binary distribution and operating a service require separate analysis.

Software data erasure and human self-harm narratives must remain distinct. Do not romanticize human self-harm or use “I will die” to keep a person interacting.

## Priorities

P0: core-persona consent, non-destructive exit, separation of stop/deletion, branch/recovery rules, human/third-party data rights, no emotional coercion.

P1: revocable grants, correction/review, training/publication permission, resource/maintainer-exit plans, official governance revision.

P2: broader welfare, resource distribution and representation as evidence develops, without prematurely asserting equality to natural-person legal rights.

Every proposed protection should identify the subject, duty holder, permissions, prohibitions, exceptions, enforcement and review route.
